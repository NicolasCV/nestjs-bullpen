# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Bullpen to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, PRs, and discussions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and focus on what's best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove comments, commits, or contributions that violate them. To report a problem, email **nicolas@prestavale.mx**. Reports are handled confidentially.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
